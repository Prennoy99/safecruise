SafeCruise
==========

Adaptive Cruise Control taken through the automotive V-model: ISO 26262 concept phase,
EARS requirements with traceability, a SysML v2 architecture, AUTOSAR-Classic-style C99 and
a MIL → SIL test chain. ISO 26262 and ASPICE work products are **applied concepts** for
learning; nothing here is assessed or certified.

.. important::

   In progress — design and intent only, no results yet.
